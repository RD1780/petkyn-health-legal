PetKyn Health Support

Your pet's data, completely private & offline.

PetKyn Health is a private, offline pet health log. Track multiple pets, attach photos, search your timeline, and keep everything stored locally on your device—no account required.

Getting Started

  1. Open the App and create your first pet profile with a name and optional photo.
  2. Use Quick Log to record appetite, energy, stool, medication, water, weight, or other alerts.
  3. Review entries in the Timeline, search by keyword, or filter by log type.
  4. Tap the circular profile icon in the header to switch pets or add another profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data uploaded to the cloud? No. PetKyn Health stores pet profiles, logs, notes, and photos locally on your device.
Do I need an account? No. The App works entirely offline after installation.
Can I track more than one pet? Yes. Each pet has a separate profile and isolated log history.
How do I add another pet? Tap the profile icon in the header, then choose + Add New Pet.
How do I edit or delete a pet? Long-press a pet on the profile selection screen, or long-press the active pet avatar in the dashboard header to edit. Deleting a pet permanently removes its logs and photos from your device.
Can I take or upload photos? Yes. Where photos are supported, choose Choose Photo or Take Photo.
Is this veterinary advice? No. PetKyn Health is a personal journal only. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for medical decisions.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera or Photos not working — Open iOS Settings → PetKyn Health → enable Camera and Photos.
  • Missing data after reinstall — Data is stored locally. Uninstalling the App removes that data unless you have a device backup that includes app data.
  • Wrong pet’s logs showing — Tap the profile icon in the header and switch to the correct pet.
  • Photo missing on web preview — Web preview stores images in browser storage. If a photo disappears after refresh, re-attach it. Native iPhone builds store photos on device normally.

Medical Disclaimer

PetKyn Health is not a medical device and does not provide veterinary diagnosis, treatment, or emergency guidance. It is intended for personal record-keeping only. If your pet may be ill or injured, contact a licensed veterinarian or emergency clinic immediately.
